# Recruiter & Reviewer Psychology

## Purpose

The portfolio is designed for multiple audiences.

Different viewers should leave with different but aligned impressions.

The goal is not only:

"He knows technologies."

The goal is:

"He thinks like an engineer who can own systems."

---

# Primary Desired Reaction

Visitors should think:

- This engineer understands systems deeply.
- This engineer can architect platforms.
- This engineer combines backend depth with product taste.
- This engineer thinks beyond tickets.
- This engineer has future technical leadership potential.

---

# Audience 1 — Technical Recruiter

## What They Look For

Recruiters scan for:

- clear role identity
- strong projects
- relevant technologies
- impact
- communication ability

---

## What They Should Notice

Within 30 seconds:

Sravan is a:

Systems Product Engineer

focused on:

- backend architecture
- distributed systems
- AI workflows
- operational platforms

---

## Avoid Recruiter Confusion

Do not appear as:

- frontend-only developer
- designer
- AI researcher
- student portfolio

---

# Audience 2 — Senior Software Engineer

## What They Look For

Senior engineers evaluate:

- architecture decisions
- tradeoffs
- debugging mindset
- scalability understanding
- ownership

---

## Signals To Show

Show:

- system boundaries
- data modeling
- state management
- APIs
- event flows
- reliability decisions
- operational constraints

---

## Impress With

Examples:

Instead of:

"I used PostgreSQL"

Explain:

"Why relational modeling and transactions mattered."

---

Instead of:

"I added AI"

Explain:

"Where semantic retrieval improved workflow efficiency."

---

Instead of:

"It scales"

Explain:

"What changes at 10x traffic."

---

# Audience 3 — Startup Founder / CTO

## What They Look For

They want builders.

Show:

- ownership
- speed
- product thinking
- ability to handle ambiguity

---

## Signals

Highlight:

- turning unclear problems into systems
- building complete products
- understanding users
- making tradeoffs

---

# Audience 4 — FAANG Engineer

## What They Look For

They care about:

- fundamentals
- system design
- correctness
- maintainability

---

Show:

- APIs
- architecture
- consistency
- performance
- testing
- scalability

---

Avoid:

- overclaiming
- fake scale
- buzzwords without explanation

---

# Audience 5 — Designer / Product Person

## What They Notice

They evaluate:

- taste
- polish
- interaction quality
- clarity

---

Show:

- intentional motion
- clean hierarchy
- premium UX
- restraint

---

# AI Positioning Rules

AI should communicate:

systems intelligence.

Good:

- RAG search
- workflow automation
- intelligent assistance
- operational insights

Bad:

- "AI-powered everything"
- generic chatbot language
- hype words

# What To Avoid

Avoid creating:

"Look at my animations."

Create:

"Look at how I think."

---

# Interview Conversion Goal

After visiting:

Recruiter:

"I want to schedule him."

Engineer:

"I want to discuss architecture with him."

Founder:

"I trust him to own a system."

Designer:

"He understands product quality."

---

# Final Principle

The portfolio should create curiosity.

The interview should confirm depth.
